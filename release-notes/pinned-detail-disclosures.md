Cast, Crew, View Artists, and Subject Tags can now be pinned open per user
across supported detail pages and future logins. Selecting a pin opens its card,
while clearing it collapses the card without preventing ordinary per-page use
of the main disclosure button. Disclosure spacing and secondary-card contrast
are also more consistent.
