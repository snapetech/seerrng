The media Associations dialog now uses the site background and presents recommendations as compact details cards. Movie and Series cards show HD and 4K availability; Music cards show MP3 and FLAC, followed by wrapped relationship text. Translucent red Cancel and green Browse More actions replace the top-right close icon, and Browse More reuses the same cards in the full explorer.
